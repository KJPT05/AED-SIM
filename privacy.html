Privacy Policy

AED Trainer · Last updated: 19 September 2026

AED Trainer is provided by Kieran Toner. This policy explains how the AED Trainer website and Android application handle information.

Summary

AED Trainer does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal information. It does not require an account, display advertising, use analytics or tracking, or request device permissions in the current release.

Information collection and use

AED Trainer does not collect personal information. You can use the app without creating an account or providing your name, email address, telephone number, location, health information, payment information, contacts, photographs, microphone recordings, or other personal data.

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, behavioural profiling, cookies for advertising or analytics, or third-party marketing software.

Data storage and sharing

AED Trainer does not save training choices, scenario results, progress, or personal data. It does not sell, share, rent, disclose, or transfer user information to third parties because it does not collect user information.

Device permissions and Bluetooth

The current release does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, notifications, Bluetooth, or other sensitive device permissions. Bluetooth-connected training hardware is not included in the current release.

Children and age groups

AED Trainer is an educational practice tool for teenagers and adults. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

External services

The app may be delivered through services such as GitHub Pages, web browsers, Google Play, or an app-store testing service. Those providers may process limited technical information necessary to deliver their services under their own privacy policies. AED Trainer itself does not receive, access, or use that information for analytics, advertising, profiling, or other purposes.

Training and safety notice

AED Trainer is a free educational practice simulation to support familiarity with adult cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) and automated external defibrillator (AED) use. It is not a replacement for accredited Basic Life Support training, professional medical advice, emergency services, clinical supervision, or the instructions supplied with a real AED.

The adult Basic Life Support and AED learning flow is designed in line with Resuscitation Council UK 2025 guidance. AED Trainer is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Resuscitation Council UK, emergency services, or any AED manufacturer.

In a real emergency, call 999 or your local emergency number. If a person is unresponsive and not breathing normally, start CPR and follow the instructions given by emergency services and the real AED.

Changes to this policy

If AED Trainer's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the relevant app update is released. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.
