ADULT BASIC LIFE SUPPORT

CPR + AED practice

Practise the first actions for an unresponsive adult who is not breathing normally. Follow the prompts and use the AED as soon as it arrives.

How it works

  1. Check that it is safe and check for a response
  2. If the person is unresponsive, call 999 immediately
  3. While the call connects, check whether they are breathing normally
  4. If breathing is abnormal, absent or you are unsure, start CPR
  5. When the AED arrives, switch it on and follow its voice prompts
  6. Attach the pads where shown, stand clear for analysis, then restart CPR immediately
